A Todo Pedal is the new design standard for bike infrastructure in Latin America. The client is Inter-American Development Bank. I was the designer of this book while worked at [Gehl Studio](http://gehlpeople.com/).

You can find the complete book at Gehl [ISSUU](https://issuu.com/gehlarchitects/docs/a-todo-pedal-guia-para-construir-ci) and Inter-American Development Bank [Publication Page](https://publications.iadb.org/handle/11319/7530).
