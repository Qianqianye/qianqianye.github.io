I translated a small part of examples of p5.js documentation to Chinese in 2018. My work 'Shan Shui II' was featured as the homepage of [p5js.org](https://p5js.org/) when it launched its Chinese version.

You can find the entire Chinese Translation for p5.js project at this [Medium](https://medium.com/processing-foundation/chinese-translation-for-p5-js-and-preparing-a-future-of-more-translations-b56843ea096e) post by 2018 Processing Foundation Fellow [Kenneth Lim](https://designerken.be/designing).
